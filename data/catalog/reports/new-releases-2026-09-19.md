# Hobbiton Piper catalog gap research — 2026-09-19

## Scope

This incremental catalog update researches the 28 apparent catalog gaps from the Hobbiton Piper transcript audit completed on 2026-09-19. The search window is historical through 2026-09-19 because several discussed blends are discontinued or renamed. `reviewedAt` records the verification date, not a release date or an availability claim.

## Sources checked

- Manufacturer and tobacconist pages: Boswell, Vauen, L. J. Peretti, and The Country Squire.
- Established retailer and distributor pages: Smokingpipes, including historical product pages and manufacturer interviews.
- Tobacco Reviews for historical house blends whose original shops or product pages are no longer available.
- The Hobbiton Piper transcript evidence was used for discovery only, not as the sole catalog source.

## Confirmed identities

Twenty-seven identities are supported by direct pages and added to the source collection. Important identity resolutions:

- `Sun Bear (2020)` is stored as Cornell & Diehl `Sun Bear`, retaining the year wording as an alias. `Sun Bear Black Locust` remains a distinct named edition.
- Scarecrow and The Haunting Cake are marketed under `Warped`, though Cornell & Diehl manufactures the blends.
- Edward G. Robinson's Pipe Blend is stored under `Sutliff`, which owns and manufactured the modern blend.
- The Cavendish in the Woods and Spring Training are stored under their marketed brand, `pipesandcigars.com`; Russ Ouellette is the blender rather than the catalog maker.
- Sleepy Hollow is a Straus Tobacco house blend, not a Sutliff product.
- The six Country Squire Fantasy South names are current display identities. Their former Middle Earth names are retained as aliases and permanent previous names.
- Tewksbury & Company's original Hobbit's Weed remains distinct from the existing 4noggins match blend.
- Sutliff Red Virginia Ribbon remains distinct from Cornell & Diehl Red Virginia Ribbon.

## Unresolved candidate

- `Most Amazing`: the transcript shows a viewer-supplied jar introduced near a Missouri Meerschaum reference, but no maker/product page, directory record, or other corroborating source was found. The phrase may be a handwritten jar label rather than a marketed blend identity. It is excluded pending a legible label image or independent product source.

## Access and evidence gaps

- Several original product pages are discontinued. For those blends, established historical retailer or Tobacco Reviews identity pages were used.
- Inclusion establishes a names-only identity. It does not establish current production, stock, recipe continuity, or release date.
